About Kasr

A Palace of Coffee
in the Heart of Egypt

We source exclusively from Brazil's most celebrated coffee regions — Minas Gerais, Cerrado, and Sul de Minas — and bring those beans directly to Egypt with zero compromise on quality, freshness, or flavour.

Whether you are a café owner, a home enthusiast, or a business looking for a reliable wholesale coffee partner, Kasr delivers the richness of Brazilian coffee culture straight to your cup.

The Brazilian Coffee Story

From the Highlands of Brazil
to Your Cup in Egypt

🌄

Minas Gerais Highlands

Brazil's most celebrated coffee region. High altitude, rich volcanic soil, and perfect temperatures produce beans with deep body, chocolate richness, and natural sweetness — the foundation of every Kasr blend.

☀️

Natural Sun Drying

Brazilian coffee cherries are dried naturally under the sun, concentrating sugars and developing that signature full-bodied, smooth flavour profile with low acidity — perfect for both espresso and filter brewing.

🔥

Precision Roasting

Each batch is carefully roasted to the ideal profile: light roasts reveal delicate fruit and floral notes, medium roasts bring out nutty caramel sweetness, and dark roasts deliver the bold intensity Egyptians love in espresso and Turkish coffee.

🏰

Delivered Across Egypt

From Cairo to Alexandria and beyond — Kasr Brazilian Coffee is delivered fresh, sealed, and ready to brew. For homes, cafés, restaurants, and offices that refuse to settle for ordinary.
